Frequently asked questions

How do I use SaveVid?
Paste a public media link, check the formats the source makes available, choose one, and follow the download prompt. Availability can vary by source, format, region, and network.
Can I download any video I find online?
No. Only save content you own or have explicit permission or another valid legal basis to save. You are responsible for following copyright law, privacy rights, and the source platform's terms. See our Acceptable Use Policy.
Why might a link or format not work?
A source may restrict access, change its service, or no longer offer that format. SaveVid cannot guarantee that every platform, link, or quality will be available. Try a format shown for that link, or check the source platform's own options.
What information does SaveVid keep?
The page keeps theme and recent-download history in your browser's local storage. Submitted links are sent to the backend to process the request; technical rate limits may use the request IP. See the Privacy Policy and Cookie and Storage Policy.
Does SaveVid belong to YouTube or other platforms?
No. SaveVid is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the platforms shown on this page. Their names and marks belong to their respective owners.

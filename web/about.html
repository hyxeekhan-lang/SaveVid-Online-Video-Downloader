About SaveVid

Last updated: October 25, 2026

SaveVid is an online utility that checks publicly accessible media links and, where supported, helps a visitor save a selected format to their own device.

How the service works

When a visitor submits a link, the service processes that link to retrieve available media information. Depending on the selected format and the visitor's connection, the request may be handled by SaveVid's hosted service or by a SaveVid backend running on the visitor's own computer. Media is delivered to the visitor; SaveVid is not intended to be a library or hosting service for downloaded videos.

Independent service

SaveVid is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or any other third-party platform. Third-party names and marks belong to their respective owners. Availability depends on the source platform, the visitor's location, and the rights and permissions applicable to the media.

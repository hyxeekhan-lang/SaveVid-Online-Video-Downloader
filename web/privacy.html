Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you use SaveVid. The service does not ask you to create an account or provide a name to check a link.

Information you submit

When you submit a media URL or request a format, the URL and format information are sent to the SaveVid backend needed to process that request. The backend may contact the source platform or media host to retrieve information and deliver the requested media. Do not submit private, confidential, or access-controlled links.

Technical information and rate limits

The service uses the request's IP address, where available from the hosting platform, to apply temporary rate limits and protect the service from abuse. The application keeps rate-limit counters in memory for approximately one minute. The hosting provider may separately process connection data or retain security and access logs under its own policies and retention practices; SaveVid does not control those provider logs.

Browser storage

SaveVid stores your chosen theme and a limited list of recent download entries in your browser's local storage. This information stays in that browser unless you clear it, clear site data, or use a different browser/device. The history is not an account and is not synchronized by SaveVid.

Downloads and temporary files

Some download methods relay media without saving a copy to SaveVid storage. Other methods may use temporary files on the machine running the backend. A locally run backend can leave downloaded files in that computer's temporary download folder until they are removed or the operating system clears them. Hosted temporary storage is controlled by the hosting environment and may be recycled. SaveVid does not promise a particular deletion time for temporary files.

Service providers and source platforms

SaveVid relies on hosting and network providers, and may request content from the third-party platform or media host named in the URL. The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical information as part of serving their services and process it under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and analytics

The current page source does not include Google Analytics or Google AdSense ad code. If Google ads are enabled, Google and its third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other sites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on those visits. Visitors can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. If other ad networks are used, the operator must identify them here and provide their privacy and opt-out information.

If advertising, analytics, or other tracking tools are added, this policy must be updated before use. For visitors in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the operator must also provide the disclosures and obtain consent required by law and Google's applicable consent policies, including use of a Google-certified consent management platform where required.

Children and data requests

SaveVid is a general utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the service does not use accounts, it may not be able to identify or retrieve a particular visitor's request history. Contact the operator for privacy questions or requests; contact details must be configured on the Contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its providers change. The date at the top indicates the latest policy revision.

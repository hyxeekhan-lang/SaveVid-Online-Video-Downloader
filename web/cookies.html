Storage used by the SaveVid page

The current page uses browser local storage (not an account) to remember your display theme and up to 30 recent download entries. You can clear these entries using the site's history controls or remove SaveVid site data in your browser settings.

Cookies and service providers

The current SaveVid page source does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting, security, or embedded third-party services may use their own technologies subject to their own policies. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive ordinary connection information when a font is requested.

Advertising and consent

If advertising or analytics tools are introduced, this page must be updated to identify them and explain their choices before they are used. Depending on the visitors and ad products served, Google may require a certified consent management platform for users in certain regions. Ad technology must not be added until the applicable consent and privacy requirements have been reviewed and implemented.

Managing storage

You can block or clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Blocking local storage may prevent theme and history preferences from being remembered. Browser or hosting-provider security controls may operate separately from SaveVid's page storage.
